# Brand logos

Put the two brand logo image files in this folder, named **exactly**:

- `salkara.png` — the Salkara Family Restaurant logo
- `marine.png` — the CISO Marine World logo

They appear at the top of every coupon.

Notes:
- PNG is preferred (JPG also works). Keep the same file names.
- The Salkara logo shows on a white background; the Marine World logo shows on
  a black background (so its white/blue lettering stays visible).
- Until these files are added, the coupon shows styled text placeholders
  automatically — nothing breaks.
